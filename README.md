### 🔥 Thesis

**_Why do iron particles fail to burn? Numerical investigations into the interaction of turbulence and iron powder combustion_**

Iron powder can be burned for heat and power, and the resulting iron oxide reduced back to iron with clean energy, closing the loop. Whether a particle in a turbulent flow ignites, burns, or quietly fails to depends on how turbulence moves it through hot and cold regions of the flow. My work uses high-fidelity simulations to understand why.

- **Methods:** DNS and LES of reacting turbulent flows, Euler–Lagrange point-particle tracking
- **Focus:** particle ignition and extinction, turbulence–particle interaction, flame structure in iron dust clouds
